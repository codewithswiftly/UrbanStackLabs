Section 01

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal data without your consent. The app may store financial data locally on your device — such as expenses, income, and categories — solely to power the app's features and improve your experience.

📷
Section 02

Camera Permission

The app requests access to your device camera solely to scan receipts and bills for expense tracking. All image processing is performed entirely on-device and images are never shared with any third parties.

🗄️
Section 03

Data Storage

All your data is stored securely on your device. If you choose to enable backup features, your data may be stored in your personal Google Drive account under your own Google credentials — we do not have access to it.

🔗
Section 04

Third-Party Services

We may use trusted services such as Google Firebase for analytics and app performance improvements. These services are carefully selected and do not collect personally identifiable financial data from your device.

🔒
Section 05

Security

We implement robust security measures including encryption and local authentication to protect your data. Your financial information is treated with the highest level of care and is never transmitted without your knowledge.

🎛️
Section 06

User Control

You have full control over your data at all times. You can delete all your data at any time directly from within the app. We believe your data belongs to you — no strings attached.

🔄
Section 07

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
