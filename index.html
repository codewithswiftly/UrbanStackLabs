The Most Important Section

Your Money. Your Data. Your Control.

BudgetCraft does not store your financial transactions on UrbanStack Labs' servers. All financial data remains on your own device, and backups are stored only in your personal Google Drive account.

You always own and control your financial data — in full, forever.
